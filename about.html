About RAFM

A Family Rooted in Christ, Growing in Faith

For 80 years, RAFM has upheld the calling to spread and fellowship in the Gospel of Jesus Christ, establish and organize the church, and live according to the pattern of the apostles.

Our Story

Celebrating 80 Years of Service

RAFM is a spiritual family committed to the Gospel of Jesus Christ. Our journey is shaped by worship, fellowship, discipleship, and faithful service — a legacy built over eight decades and carried forward with reverence and hope.

We exist to help people encounter Christ, grow in biblical truth, and find belonging in a community that prays together, serves together, and walks in the pattern entrusted to the apostles.

Whether you are seeking faith, returning to church, or looking for a place to serve, there is room for you in the family.

80 YearsOf faithful worship, fellowship, and service in Christ.

Mission & Values

What We Uphold

Our calling is lived out through the Gospel, the life of the church, and a pattern of faithfulness that shapes how we worship, gather, and serve.

The Gospel

We are devoted to spreading and fellowshipping in the Gospel of Jesus Christ, proclaiming truth with grace and living it with conviction.

The Church

We gather to establish and organize the church as a spiritual home — a place of worship, discipleship, care, and shared purpose.

Apostolic Pattern

We seek to live according to the pattern of the apostles, grounded in Scripture, prayer, fellowship, and faithful obedience.

“They devoted themselves to the apostles’ teaching and to fellowship, to the breaking of bread and to prayer.”
Acts 2:42
